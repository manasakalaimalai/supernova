FROM THE FOUNDER
A room that
NEVER EXISTED

Take the hundred most ambitious female builders in India and put them in one room. Sounds simple, right? But that room has never existed. Not once.

Until October 10th, 2026.

We created Supernova because women founders face a version of entrepreneurship that the ecosystem barely acknowledges. Getting in front of the right investors can take months, if it happens at all. Finding the co-founder who gets it, the operator who's solved your exact problem, the peer who's three steps ahead? That's often left to chance.

Supernova is the front door to Nova Residency, India's first residency for women founders. We're backing AI-native women in their 20s, giving them a month to build together, ship together, and reach Demo Day with something real. But before the residency, there's Supernova: one day, 100 founders, 20+ investors, and the conversations that usually take six months or never happen.

This is just the beginning. See you at Edition 2.

POURING TONIGHT

200 people stayed for the after party: the 100 founders plus 100 more from the ecosystem. The investors who came stayed, and they genuinely got along with everybody. The conversations continued, deeper now, outside the structure of the day.

Friendships formed. Co-founders met. Operators who'd solved the same problem three years ago shared what worked. Follow-up meetings were scheduled. WhatsApp groups spun up. The momentum from the day carried into the night.

Supernova Edition 1 was more than an event. It was proof that the room we needed has always been possible. We just had to build it. See you at Edition 2.
